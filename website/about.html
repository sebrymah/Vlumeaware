About

Security awareness, built for how teams actually work.

Vlumeaware is a product of Vlumetech LTD. We build and run offensive-security and awareness services, and we kept seeing the same thing wherever we worked. The tooling was built somewhere else, the lures were written for somewhere else, and compliance was an afterthought. A pretext that lands in Lagos is not the one that lands in Nairobi or Manila. So we built the platform we wished existed: one that speaks the local language of fraud, in whichever market it is pointed at.

📍

Local by design

Invoice and mandate fraud, CBN/FIRS notices, payroll scams — the attacks your staff will really face, not a US template library.

⚖️

Compliance-first

A consent gate, domain verification and query-layer isolation, so an NDPA-answerable CISO can deploy it with confidence.

🤝

Part of a bigger practice

Vlumeaware sits alongside Vlumetech’s VAPT and Tactful Consult engagements — awareness that plugs into real security work.

Our approach

Measure human risk. Then reduce it — automatically.

Awareness only matters if it changes behaviour. Every part of Vlumeaware is built to close the gap between a risky click and the training that fixes it, and to prove — with certificates and scores — that it worked.
