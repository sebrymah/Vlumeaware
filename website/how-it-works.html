Phase 1 · Setup

Prove ownership, load your people.

🛡️

1 · Verify your domains

Add a DNS TXT record to prove you own each domain. Only verified domains can be onboarded or emailed — so you can never phish outsiders.

👥

2 · Build your roster

Add employees one at a time or by CSV, with department and manager. Existing people update instead of duplicating; seats are counted per plan.

📝

3 · Sign authorization

A signed authorization is recorded before anything sends — the consent gate that keeps you on the right side of the NDPA.

Phase 2 · Run

Simulate, teach, and measure — repeatedly.

  • Pick a scenario. Clone one from the local catalogue, let Vlumeaware AI draft one, or write your own. Vlumetech reviews and approves it before it can go out.
  • Launch a campaign. One tracked send per employee. Schedule it, pace it over a window, or fire immediately — and halt everything with the kill switch.
  • Watch the signals. Opens, clicks, credential submissions and reports all attach to each send, giving recipient-level truth.
  • Teach on click. A click lands the employee on a page that names the red flags and plays the matching awareness video.
  • Assess & certify. A short quiz follows; a pass marks training complete and issues a certificate.
  • Remediate the risky. Repeat clickers surface on the risk board and can be auto-enrolled into targeted remediation.
