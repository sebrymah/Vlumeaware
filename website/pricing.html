Questions

Pricing FAQ

What currency am I billed in?

Rates are quoted in USD by default. We also bill in local currency in the markets we operate in, including Naira, and we price to local purchasing power rather than charging one global rate. Tell us where your team sits and we will quote your region.

What counts as an “employee”?

Every person on your roster who can receive a simulation. Updating an existing person doesn’t consume an extra seat; only new people do.

Do I need my own email infrastructure?

No. Vlumeaware sends simulations for you from a dedicated sending identity, kept separate from your corporate domain’s reputation. You verify the recipient domains you own.

Is the ISO 27001 content really included on Starter?

Yes — all ten topics (video, quiz and phishing simulation each) are on every tier. Higher tiers add risk scoring, custom authoring and export.

Can this be bundled with a VAPT engagement?

Yes. Enterprise engagements are commonly bundled with Vlumetech’s VAPT and Tactful Consult services. Ask us for a combined scope.
