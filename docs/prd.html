01

Executive summary

What Vlumeaware is, and why it exists.

Vlumeaware runs authorized phishing simulations against a client's own employees, measures who opens, clicks, reports, and submits credentials, and then auto-routes anyone who falls for a lure into the exact training module that addresses that attack — closing the loop from exposure to remediation without a human in the middle.

It is a paid add-on to Vlumetech's security-awareness curriculum and VAPT services, not a standalone tool. It differentiates from KnowBe4, Proofpoint, Cofense and SoSafe on three axes: NDPA/NDPR compliance baked into the workflow, Nigerian business context (invoice- and mandate-fraud lures, local payroll cycles, CBN/FIRS notice formats), and a closed training feedback loop grounded in an ISO 27001:2022 content pack.

The platform is multi-tenant from the ground up: every client's data is isolated at the database-query layer, not merely in application logic. Two gates protect against misuse — a signed-authorization consent gate blocks any simulation for an unapproved client, and a domain-ownership gate ensures a client can only ever phish addresses on a domain it has proven it owns. Clients self-serve onto a 7-day trial; Vlumetech staff approve them into standing, licensed accounts.

The measurable unit is a send — one tracked email to one employee. Every event (open, click, report, credential submission, quiz pass) writes back to that row, which is what makes recipient-level reporting and per-person risk scoring possible.

02

Problem & opportunity

Why a locally-grounded simulator, and why now.

Phishing is the dominant initial-access vector for Nigerian SMEs, and the lures that land are local: vendor bank-mandate changes timed to the monthly invoice run, fake CBN/FIRS re-verification notices, payroll-account confirmations. Global awareness platforms train against US-centric templates and treat compliance as an afterthought — an awkward fit for an organisation answerable to the NDPA.

Compliance-first — consent gate blocks unauthorized sends Domain-bound — can only phish addresses you own Local context — invoice, payroll, CBN/FIRS lures Closed loop — a click auto-assigns the fixing module ISO‑aligned — ten Annex A awareness topics ready to run

The opportunity is to be the awareness platform a Nigerian mid-market CISO can put in front of their board and their regulator with equal confidence.

03

Goals & non-goals

Deliberately narrow, defensively built.

Goals

  • Provably isolate every client's data at the query layer.
  • Make it impossible to simulate without signed authorization or to email anyone off a verified domain.
  • Auto-route failures into targeted training; measure completion with certificates.
  • Give Vlumetech staff a curated, reusable catalogue of scenarios, videos and quizzes.
  • Let clients self-serve onto a trial and staff approve them into licensed accounts.

Non-goals (v1)

  • Not a SIEM or email gateway — it simulates and measures, it does not filter real mail.
  • No real-time SOC / incident-response integration.
  • No storage of what an employee types on a simulated login page (metadata only).
  • Not multi-language yet (English only in v1).
Scope evolved during buildSeveral items were promoted to first-class features on request: self-serve signup, a content-authoring stack (template catalogue, manual composer, quizzes, shared libraries), and domain-ownership verification. All are documented below.

05

The core loop

From an approved scenario to a measured, remediated employee.

  1. Author or clone a scenario. A client clones one from the global catalogue (or Claude drafts one), edits it, and submits it for review.
  2. Vlumetech approves. Only an approved scenario can be attached to a live campaign.
  3. Launch a campaign. One send is materialised per employee per scenario — but only for employees on a verified domain.
  4. Track. Opens (pixel), clicks (redirect to a simulated landing page), credential submissions and report-a-phish actions all write back to the send.
  5. Teach. A click lands the employee on a teachable-moment page that names the scenario's red flags and plays the assigned awareness video.
  6. Assess. A short quiz follows; a pass marks the employee's training complete and issues a certificate.
  7. Remediate. Repeat clickers are surfaced on the risk board and can be auto-enrolled into a remediation module.
06

Feature specification

Each block lists its acceptance criteria.

Employee onboarding & domain verification New

A client proves it owns a domain via a DNS TXT record before it can onboard anyone on it — so no one outside the organisation is ever phished.

  • Add multiple domains; each gets a unique token and a TXT record to publish at _vlumeaware.<domain>.
  • Verify performs a live DNS lookup and flips the domain to verified.
  • Adding employees (single or CSV) rejects any address not on a verified domain, with a clear reason.
  • Campaign launch filters recipients to verified domains — a second guard, so a stale row can't leak out.
  • Single-employee add and bulk-CSV upload; existing rows update rather than duplicate; seat limits enforced.

Scenario catalogue & approvals Staff + client

A global library of phishing scenarios that staff author and clients clone.

  • Staff add / edit / delete catalogue scenarios (subject, spoofed sender, HTML body, red flags, difficulty).
  • Clients clone a catalogue scenario or generate one with Claude, then edit it.
  • Every client scenario is unapproved until Vlumetech reviews it; the approvals queue shows a cross-tenant list with an email preview and approve / reject.
  • Placeholders {{EMPLOYEE_NAME}} and {{TRACKING_URL}} are substituted at send time.

Campaigns & simulation Client

The measurement engine.

  • Create a campaign, attach one or more approved scenarios, select employees, launch now or schedule.
  • A preflight checklist gates launch (employees present, approved scenario attached, consent on file).
  • Sends are queued through Redis/BullMQ; a kill switch halts an active campaign.
  • Tracking endpoints record open, click, credential submission and report; report only credits a catch when it precedes a click.
  • Emails send via SES when configured; otherwise a log mailer records them for safe testing.

Awareness training & shared video library Staff + client

Video modules a client uploads or links, plus a global library staff curate.

  • Add a module by hosted link or by uploading a video file (stored in Supabase Storage, playable https URL).
  • Preview plays any module — upload or link — before it goes out.
  • Clients clone modules from the shared Vlumetech library into their own.
  • The teachable-moment page serves the assigned module to the employee who clicked.

Quizzes & shared quiz library New

Knowledge checks that gate training completion, with a reusable global library.

  • Staff publish shared quizzes; clients clone one onto a training module or author their own.
  • Questions via a form, bulk JSON, or CSV import; correct answers and explanations per question.
  • Scored server-side; a pass at the configured mark marks training complete and issues a certificate.
  • Per-quiz results show each employee's score and pass/fail.

Reporting, risk & certificates Client + staff

  • Campaign reporting: open, click, report and submission rates.
  • Per-employee risk scoring; a repeat-clicker list with one-click auto-enrol into a remediation module.
  • Certificates issued on quiz pass; a Reported view lists caught simulations.
  • Staff cross-client overview and an append-only audit log.

Multi-tenant, trial & licensing Platform

  • Self-serve signup creates a 7-day trial tenant capped at 20 seats; staff approve into a licensed tier with a seat limit.
  • A writable-tenant guard blocks creation and sending for unapproved / lapsed / suspended tenants.
  • Deliverability settings (sending domain, allowlist) recorded per tenant.

Technical architecture

A monorepo, deployed entirely on free tiers.

API NestJS (Node 22) · Prisma 6 Web Next.js 16 · React 19 · Tailwind DB PostgreSQL (Supabase) Queue BullMQ · Redis Storage Supabase Storage AI Claude (scenario drafting) Mail AWS SES / log mailer
  • Web (Next.js) on Vercel — the two consoles and the public teachable-moment / simulated-login pages.
  • API + worker (NestJS, one Docker image) on Render — the API serves requests; the worker drains the send and scheduler queues.
  • Database on Supabase Postgres (session pooler), with a tenant-guard Prisma extension enforcing isolation and RLS enabled on every table.
  • Redis (Render Key Value) backs BullMQ for send scheduling and send-window pacing.
  • Object storage in a public Supabase bucket for awareness videos, returning playable https URLs.
Known free-tier constraintsRender's free web service cold-starts after idle (~50 s first request) and has an ephemeral disk; video storage was moved to Supabase for durability. Removing cold-starts and adding persistent Redis + backups is on the roadmap.

Security & compliance

Isolation and consent are structural, not conventions.

  • Query-layer tenant isolation. A Prisma extension narrows every read and write to the tenant in async context; a superadmin-only runAsSystem escape hatch is the sole cross-tenant path, and it is audited.
  • Consent gate. No simulation runs for a tenant without a signed authorization agreement and an approved status.
  • Domain-ownership gate. Employees can only be onboarded and emailed on DNS-verified domains — enforced at both add and send.
  • Row-level security is enabled on every table; the application connects as a role that bypasses RLS, giving defence in depth against a direct-DB mistake.
  • Credential capture is metadata-only — the simulated login records that a submission happened, never the plaintext an employee typed.
  • Auth is JWT with per-portal role checks and rate-limited sign-in; separate staff and client entry points.
Open security actionsRotate the database password exposed during setup and replace the seeded demo passwords before onboarding real clients. Move uploaded-video storage to a private bucket with signed URLs when reputational/PII sensitivity increases.
12

Non-functional requirements

Reliability & performance

  • Sends are pre-materialised before any job runs, so reporting has a complete denominator and the kill switch has something to halt.
  • Send-window pacing spreads a campaign over a configurable interval.
  • Cold-start and ephemeral-disk limits of the free tier are documented and slated for removal.

Deliverability & testability

  • The log mailer lets the full click→training→quiz path be tested without sending real email.
  • SPF/DKIM/DMARC posture for the sending domain is a planned pre-flight check.
  • A dedicated sending identity keeps simulation traffic off the corporate domain's reputation.
